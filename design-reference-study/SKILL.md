---
name: design-reference-study
description: Extract the reusable design DNA of a supplied screenshot, site, or interface reference without blindly cloning its content or brand.
---
Use when the user asks to study, reverse-engineer, learn from, or derive a portable design direction from a reference.

1. Separate structure from branding: layout grammar, density, type scale, spacing, surfaces, border/radius logic, icon treatment, motion, and content hierarchy.
2. Identify signature moves versus generic conventions.
3. Capture what makes the reference coherent: alignment rules, repetition, contrast, pacing, and component relationships.
4. Do not copy logos, protected assets, unique copy, or proprietary brand identity unless the user is authorized to use them.
5. Translate observations into abstract tokens/patterns rather than pixel-by-pixel mimicry.
6. Note what should *not* be transferred because it conflicts with the target product, accessibility, platform conventions, or performance.
7. Produce a compact portable brief: principles, tokens, layout grammar, component cues, motion behavior, and anti-patterns.
8. When adapting the result, preserve the target product's domain vocabulary and information architecture.

The goal is to learn the reference's system, not impersonate the reference.
