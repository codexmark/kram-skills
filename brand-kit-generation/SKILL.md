---
name: brand-kit-generation
description: Generate a practical brand-kit concept covering logo direction, color system, typography, iconography, imagery, voice, and sample applications without pretending preliminary concepts are final trademark-safe identity assets.
---
Use when the user asks for a brand board, visual identity direction, brand kit, or early branding system.

1. Start from brand purpose, audience, personality, category conventions, differentiation, and usage contexts.
2. Generate a small number of distinct concepts with a clear rationale for each.
3. Define color roles, type pairing, logo/mark direction, icon/illustration treatment, imagery mood, spacing/radius character, and tone of voice.
4. Show how the system behaves on representative surfaces such as app UI, website, social graphic, document, or packaging when relevant.
5. Distinguish concept exploration from finalized logo artwork and trademark clearance.
6. Avoid superficial tech clichés unless intentionally requested.
7. Check accessibility when brand colors are proposed for interfaces.
8. Once a direction is selected, hand it to `brand-guidelines` / `theme-factory` for enforceable implementation rules.

A brand kit should create a repeatable visual language, not just a logo plus five hex codes.
