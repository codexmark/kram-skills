---
name: brand-guidelines
description: Translate brand identity into enforceable product/interface rules for color, typography, imagery, iconography, voice, spacing, and visual behavior.
---
Use when a product needs a brand system, when an existing brand must be applied consistently, or when several surfaces drift apart visually.

1. Separate immutable brand assets from adaptable product UI decisions.
2. Define brand essentials: logo use, color roles, typography, imagery/illustration, icon style, tone of voice, and prohibited treatments.
3. Convert visual intent into semantic tokens and component-level guidance instead of one-off mockups.
4. Define how brand behaves in light/dark/high-contrast contexts and on dense operational surfaces.
5. Protect accessibility: brand colors may require adjusted shades for text, focus, and interactive states.
6. Distinguish marketing expression from product utility; a campaign can be more expressive than an admin table without becoming a different brand.
7. Include examples of correct and incorrect usage.
8. Record exceptions and co-branding rules explicitly.
9. Keep implementation references tied to the canonical design system.

A useful brand guideline reduces future ambiguity; it should tell another agent what not to invent.
