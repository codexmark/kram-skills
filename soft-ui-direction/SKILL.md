---
name: soft-ui-direction
description: Create calm, premium interfaces using restrained contrast, generous spacing, subtle depth, quiet motion, and refined typography without sacrificing clarity.
---
Use when the user asks for a soft, calm, luxurious, elegant, or comfortable visual direction.

1. Use generous whitespace and a small number of large compositional groups.
2. Keep contrast comfortable but accessibility-compliant; "soft" never means low-contrast text.
3. Prefer subtle borders/surface shifts over heavy shadows.
4. Use restrained, high-quality typography and avoid excessive weights/sizes.
5. Keep the palette narrow, with one controlled accent family.
6. Use gentle motion for continuity and feedback, not spectacle.
7. Avoid rounded-everything, frosted-glass-everywhere, beige luxury clichés, and decorative gradients without purpose.
8. Preserve strong task hierarchy and visible actions.
9. Verify that the design still works for dense content and small screens if the product requires them.

Softness should reduce visual stress, not reduce information clarity.
