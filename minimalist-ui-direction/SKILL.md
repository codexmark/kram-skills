---
name: minimalist-ui-direction
description: Build restrained editorial/product interfaces with strong structure, typography, alignment, whitespace, and very limited decorative vocabulary.
---
Use when the user wants minimalist, Linear/Notion-like, editorial, quiet, or highly structured UI.

1. Make alignment and spacing do most of the visual work.
2. Limit type sizes, weights, colors, radii, shadows, and icon styles.
3. Prefer borders, dividers, whitespace, and hierarchy over nested cards.
4. Use one dominant action and de-emphasize secondary controls.
5. Keep empty space intentional; do not confuse minimalism with missing information.
6. Use color primarily for action, state, and limited emphasis.
7. Avoid gratuitous gradients, illustration, glass, and decorative motion.
8. Keep content width appropriate to reading vs operational tasks.
9. Verify that critical states remain obvious when decoration is stripped away.
10. Minimalism must survive accessibility and real data, not just empty mockups.
