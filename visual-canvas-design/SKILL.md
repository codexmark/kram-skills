---
name: visual-canvas-design
description: Plan standalone visual artifacts such as posters, social graphics, one-pagers, diagrams, covers, banners, and presentation-like compositions rather than application UI.
---
Use when the output is a visual composition, not an interactive software screen.

1. Establish artifact dimensions, medium, viewing distance, export needs, and content hierarchy first.
2. Pick one composition concept and a clear focal point.
3. Define grid, margins, typography, palette, imagery/illustration treatment, and contrast before decorative details.
4. Treat text as content to be read, not texture; preserve legibility at actual output size.
5. Use brand assets and logos according to their rules; do not distort or improvise protected marks.
6. Respect print/screen differences such as bleed, safe area, resolution, and color constraints when relevant.
7. Keep diagrams semantically truthful; never beautify away important distinctions.
8. Produce variants only when they represent meaningful layout or hierarchy alternatives.
9. Verify the final composition at target size and, for print, with appropriate margins/bleed.

Do not apply application-navigation or component-system conventions to a standalone graphic unless they genuinely help the composition.
