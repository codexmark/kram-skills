---
name: anti-slop-interface-design
description: Detect and remove generic AI-design fingerprints while preserving the product's real brand, usability, and design-system constraints.
---
Use when a UI looks templated, synthetic, overdecorated, or interchangeable with common AI-generated interfaces.

Audit for:
- purple/blue gradients used without brand reason;
- identical icon-card grids;
- excessive glass, pills, radius, shadows, and glow;
- every section centered;
- arbitrary badges and fake metrics;
- decorative motion with no interaction purpose;
- copy that sounds generic or promotional inside an operational product;
- repeated SaaS page formulas regardless of content.

Workflow:
1. Identify the product's authentic visual language and task hierarchy.
2. Remove decoration before adding new decoration.
3. Distill containers: fewer cards, fewer borders, clearer grouping.
4. Quiet secondary elements and strengthen one real focal point.
5. Replace generic copy and icons with domain-specific language and meaningful affordances.
6. Preserve deliberate brand choices even if they resemble a common pattern; intent matters more than novelty.
7. Recheck accessibility and responsive behavior after simplification.

The goal is not to make every interface unusual. It is to make every decision look intentional.
