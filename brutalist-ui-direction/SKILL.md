---
name: brutalist-ui-direction
description: Create deliberate brutalist or neo-brutalist interfaces with exposed structure, high contrast, strong typography, hard edges, and intentionally raw composition.
---
Use only when the user explicitly asks for brutalism, neo-brutalism, raw editorial structure, or a similarly forceful visual language.

1. Make the structural idea explicit: grid, blocks, borders, typography, and alignment should carry the identity.
2. Use contrast intentionally and keep text accessibility-compliant.
3. Limit the palette; high contrast is stronger when not diluted by many colors.
4. Prefer hard edges, visible outlines, and direct spacing over soft depth effects.
5. Treat type as architecture: scale, weight, and line breaks matter more than decorative icons.
6. Avoid fake terminal/hacker motifs unless the brief calls for them.
7. Keep controls predictable even when visual styling is unconventional.
8. Do not let visual aggression hide state, hierarchy, or destructive consequences.
9. Verify mobile wrapping and focus states carefully; brutalist layouts can break badly when simply stacked.
10. Distinguish deliberate rawness from unfinished CSS.
